Scream maker

Builds scream.wav: a soft music box for LEAD seconds, then a synthesised scream. The site currently uses only the music box from this, with a recorded scream spliced in at LEAD:
ffmpeg -i scream.wav -i recorded.wav -filter_complex "[0:a]atrim=0:8.6,asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS[m];[1:a]atrim=START:START+3.4,asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS,afade=t=out:st=3.05:d=0.35,alimiter=limit=0.95:level=false[s];[m][s]concat=n=2:v=0:a=1,highpass=f=40" -codec:a libmp3lame -b:a 192k scream.mp3
where START is where the recorded scream begins (0.465 s in the current one).
